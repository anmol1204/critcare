📏 DLT depth & confirmation
  • Depth of DLT (cm) ≈ height (cm)/10 + 12.
  • A left-sided DLT is usually preferred (longer bronchial margin of safety); confirm placement clinically and with fibreoptic bronchoscopy (FOB) — and re-check after turning the patient to the lateral position (tubes migrate).
1

Positioning — lateral decubitus

  • Turn to the lateral position; re-check DLT position with FOB after the turn.
  • Avoid pressure-related complications — eyes, pinna, cervical spine, hyperabduction of the arm (brachial plexus), pressure areas, the down-side neurovascular bundle.
