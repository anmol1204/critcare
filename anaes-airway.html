🫁 Airway Management

"You cannot manage what you cannot picture. This topic is deliberately split into short, focused pages — one idea per tab — so you can read the airway the way you actually meet it at the bedside: anatomy, then assessment and scores, then the devices, the difficult situations, and rapid sequence induction. Move through the tabs above, or jump straight to what you need."

How to use this topic — CritCare.in Airway pilot.

Each card below opens as its own page (the same tabs sit at the top of every page, so you never lose your place). This keeps each read short instead of one very long scroll.

🫀01 · ANATOMY

Anatomy of the Airway

Upper & lower airway, laryngeal cartilages, an in-depth innervation table, the bronchial tree (why the right bronchus is more vertical) and a paediatric-vs-adult comparison.

 🔎02 · ASSESSMENT

Airway Assessment

History and every bedside examination test — Mallampati, 3-3-2, thyromental & sternomental distance, upper-lip-bite, neck movement — with photos and prediction mnemonics.

 📊03 · SCORES

Airway Scores

Original and modified Mallampati, an upgraded Cormack–Lehane grade, and the composite scores (Wilson, El-Ganzouri/SARI, LEMON, Arné) with thresholds.

 🧰04 · DEVICES

Devices & Definitions

What each term means — face mask, oro-/nasopharyngeal airway, SGA/LMA, tracheal tube — and the definitions of difficult mask ventilation, laryngoscopy, intubation and CICO.

 🩸05 · TRAUMA

Trauma & Pathology

The injured and the pathological airway — maxillofacial and laryngeal trauma, burns/inhalation, C-spine, and obstructing pathology (tumour, infection, OSMF, angioedema).

 ⚡06 · RSI

Rapid Sequence Induction

RSI as its own topic — indications, the classic sequence, drugs and doses, and Sellick's cricoid-pressure manoeuvre with the modern evidence and controversy.

 🔊07 · ULTRASOUND

Airway Ultrasound

Anterior-neck sonoanatomy, real labelled POCUS images, cricothyroid-membrane marking and tube-placement confirmation (double-tract sign).

 🎓08 · PEARLS

Advances, Mistakes & Pearls

Recent advances (VL, HFNO, eFONA), the classic mistakes, viva-ready exam pearls, and references & image credits.
