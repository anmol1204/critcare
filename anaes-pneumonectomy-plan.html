🗺️ Anaesthetic plan

General anaesthesia with controlled ventilation + thoracic epidural analgesia (TEA).

  • NPO; informed consent; premedication — midazolam may be given; aspiration prophylaxis.
Monitoring
SpO₂, ECG, NIBP, temperature, urine output, EtCO₂; invasive arterial line (IBP) for beat-to-beat pressure and repeated ABGs; CVP on the side of the thoracotomy (so a line complication doesn't risk a bilateral pneumothorax); and neuromuscular monitoring.
1

Induction

  • Two IV lines; IV or inhalational technique with short-acting agents for rapid emergence and recovery.
  • Pre-oxygenate → propofol + fentanyl + muscle relaxant → lung isolation (next tab).
