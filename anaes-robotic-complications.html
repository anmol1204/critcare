👁️ 2 · POVL

Post-operative Visual Loss (POVL)

The steep-Trendelenburg ocular hazard
Prolonged steep head-down tilt
↑ Intra-ocular pressure & orbital/ocular venous congestionWorsened by long operative time, hypotension, anaemia & large-volume crystalloid
↓ Optic-nerve perfusion → ischaemic optic neuropathy → POVL

Mitigation: limit the duration of extreme tilt, avoid hypotension & anaemia, use a restrictive fluid strategy, protect & pad the eyes, and consider intermittent levelling in very long cases.

💨 3 · Gas & Airway

CO₂-Related & Airway Complications

CO₂ / gas complications

  • Subcutaneous emphysema, pneumothorax, pneumomediastinum from CO₂ tracking
  • Hypercarbia & respiratory acidosis from absorption
  • CO₂ (gas) embolism — sudden ↓ EtCO₂, hypotension, desaturation

Airway & cerebral

  • Laryngeal / facial / conjunctival oedema → post-extubation obstruction risk
  • Cerebral oedema → delayed emergence
  • Endobronchial migration of the ETT after positioning
🦾 4 · Positioning

Position-Related Injuries

The steep tilt + prolonged immobility + shoulder supports risk peripheral nerve injuries (notably brachial plexus from shoulder braces, and lower-limb nerves in lithotomy), pressure injuries over bony prominences, and corneal abrasion. Meticulous padding, correct support placement (avoid direct brachial-plexus pressure) and eye protection are the defence — and none of it can be adjusted once the robot is docked.
