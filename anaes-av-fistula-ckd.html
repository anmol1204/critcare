⚡ 2 · The Dangerous Numbers

Metabolic Derangements

DerangementAnaesthetic relevance
HyperkalaemiaThe peri-operative danger — arrhythmia/arrest risk. Check K⁺; dialyse pre-op if elevated; avoid suxamethonium if K⁺ already high (it raises K⁺ by ~0.5 mmol/L); avoid K⁺-rich fluids
AnaemiaChronic, well-compensated (↑ 2,3-DPG, right-shifted ODC) — usually tolerated; avoid unnecessary transfusion (sensitisation before possible transplant)
Metabolic acidosisReduces protein binding (↑ free drug fraction) and shifts K⁺ out of cells
Fluid statusFluid overload (missed dialysis) or hypovolaemia (over-dialysed, below "dry weight") — assess carefully
Uraemic platelet dysfunctionBleeding tendency (± residual heparin from recent dialysis, and anticoagulants); consider DDAVP if bleeding
🫀 3 · Systemic Disease

Cardiovascular & Systemic Burden

Cardiovascular

  • Hypertension, LVH, ischaemic heart disease, uraemic pericarditis
  • Autonomic neuropathy → labile blood pressure, blunted compensation
  • Often a chronic high-output state (anaemia + existing fistulae)

Other systems

  • Delayed gastric emptying (uraemia, diabetes, autonomic) → aspiration risk
  • Diabetes is a common cause — with its own neuropathy & vascular disease
  • Immunosuppression & infection risk; difficult vascular access
