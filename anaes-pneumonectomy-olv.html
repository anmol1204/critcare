💨 5 · One-Lung Ventilation

One-Lung Ventilation, Maintenance & Fluids

🌫️ Maintenance
  • < 1 MAC volatile (preserves hypoxic pulmonary vasoconstriction); N₂O avoided (it raises PVR and expands air spaces).
OLV ventilator settingTarget
FiO₂Up to ~0.95 (titrate to SpO₂)
Tidal volume5–6 mL/kg (lung-protective)
Respiratory rate12–20/min, targeting EtCO₂ ~32–38 mmHg
ModePressure-controlled; peak airway pressure <35, plateau <25 cmH₂O
PEEP5–10 cmH₂O to the ventilated lung; permissive hypercapnia accepted

Timing & troubleshooting: OLV is started before insertion of the thoracotomy trocar/incision. If hypercarbia develops, increase minute ventilation. For hypoxaemia on OLV: confirm DLT position (FOB), ↑ FiO₂, apply PEEP to the dependent lung and CPAP to the operative lung, and — as a last resort — ask the surgeon to clamp the pulmonary artery.

💧 Fluid management
  • Judicious fluids — crystalloids < 3 L in the first 24 h; do not replace "third-space" losses.
  • Over-transfusion → oedema of the dependent lung and ↑ pulmonary shunting → post-pneumonectomy pulmonary oedema (often fatal).
