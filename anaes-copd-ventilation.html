🔁 2 · Auto-PEEP

Dynamic Hyperinflation & Auto-PEEP

How auto-PEEP (intrinsic PEEP) develops
Expiratory airflow limitation + ↓ elastic recoilThe lung cannot empty fully before the next breath
Part of each tidal volume is retained — "breath-stacking"Progressive hyperinflation; end-expiratory lung volume rises above FRC
Positive alveolar pressure at end-expiration = auto-PEEP (PEEPi)↑ work of breathing / trigger load, ↓ venous return & LV filling → hypotension; barotrauma risk (bullae)

Detecting air-trapping intra-op: capnograph CO₂ that up-slopes and never plateaus before the next breath; an expiratory flow trace that doesn't return to zero before the next breath; a rising measured PEEP; and a fall in BP as hyperinflation worsens.

🛠️ 3 · Strategy

Lung-Protective Ventilation in COPD

🎯 Goals — reduce air-trapping, avoid hypercapnia's harm, maintain oxygenation
  • Low tidal volume 6–8 mL/kg (ideal body weight)
  • Prolonged expiratory time — I:E ~1:2.5 to 1:3 (or longer); a higher inspiratory flow shortens inspiration and gives more time to exhale
  • Low respiratory rate (avoid dynamic hyperinflation)
  • FiO₂ titrated to SpO₂ > ~90%; monitor auto-PEEP
  • Plateau pressure < 30 cmH₂O (barotrauma/bullae → pneumothorax risk)
  • Accept permissive hypercapnia (as long as pH > ~7.25)
  • Pressure-controlled (decelerating flow) ventilation often gives lower peak pressures & better distribution
➕ 4 · External PEEP

How to Counter Auto-PEEP

Ventilator manoeuvres

  • Lower TV & RR; allow more expiratory time (I:E to 1:3–1:5)
  • Shorten inspiratory time (↑ peak flow) to free up exhalation time
  • Treat bronchospasm at its source

Role of external PEEP

  • Counteracts intrinsic PEEP — matches the hidden pressure the patient must overcome to trigger, ↓ trigger load & muscle fatigue
  • Splints open unstable airways in expiration → lets more air empty, ↓ hyperinflation, improves synchrony & gas exchange
The "70% rule" for external PEEP
Use low external PEEP (~3–5 cmH₂O), kept to about 70–85% of the measured intrinsic PEEP. Too much external PEEP adds to hyperinflation rather than relieving it → higher pressures & hypotension. Titrate & monitor
🫧 5 · Bronchospasm

Intra-op Bronchospasm

Recognise: ↑ peak airway pressure, rhonchi/absent breath sounds, prolonged expiration, a "shark-fin" up-sloping capnograph, ↓ tidal volume & SpO₂. Differentials: light anaesthesia, airway manipulation, endobronchial intubation, ETT obstruction/kink/cuff herniation, aspiration, pneumothorax, pulmonary oedema/embolism, and anaphylaxis.

StepAction
ImmediateDeepen anaesthesia; 100% O₂; if severe, ask surgeon to stop & remove stimulus; check tube & circuit (block/misplacement)
1st lineSalbutamol — MDI ≥ 6–8 puffs (repeat) or nebulised 5 mg
2nd lineIV hydrocortisone 2 mg/kg; nebulised ipratropium 0.5 mg; magnesium ~5 mg/kg (≈2 g) over 20 min; ketamine bolus/infusion
If anaphylaxisAdrenaline first-line — IV 10–100 µg titrated (or nebulised 1:1000)
