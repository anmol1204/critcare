A well-chosen brachial plexus block sidesteps most of it, provides a sympathectomy that helps the fistula, and protects the airway the patient doesn't need instrumented."

📘 1 · The Problem

Anaesthesia for AV Fistula Creation

🎯 The case

The creation of an arteriovenous fistula (radiocephalic "Brescia–Cimino" at the wrist, or brachiocephalic at the elbow) is the commonest vascular-access operation in end-stage renal disease for long-term haemodialysis. It is a short, superficial upper-limb procedure — but performed on a patient with the full burden of chronic kidney disease.

🧠 The one-line summary
  • Optimise the potassium and volume status (dialyse the day before, not immediately before), respect the altered drug handling of renal failure, favour a regional (brachial plexus) block — which also gives a helpful sympathectomy — and protect the fistula arm from cuffs, cannulae and hypotension.
